👋 Introduction

I'm T.S. Shri Varsha, a 3rd year B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at Arunachala College of Engineering for Women, Chennai. I discovered my love for programming in high school when I wrote my first HTML page, and haven't stopped building since. My long-term goal is to build scalable, impactful technology — ideally in the developer tools or EdTech space.
